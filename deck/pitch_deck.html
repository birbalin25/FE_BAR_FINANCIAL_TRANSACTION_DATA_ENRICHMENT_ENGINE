The solution

AI that identifies the merchant — and knows when to ask a human

Each transaction flows through a four-step engine. If a confirmed answer already exists, it reuses it instantly — no AI spend.

STEP 1

Understand

GenAI reads the raw descriptor and separates facilitator, merchant name, location & store number.

→
STEP 2

Shortlist

Vector search retrieves the 10 most similar merchants from the governed merchant master.

→
STEP 3

Decide

An LLM judge weighs name and location and returns Matched, No match, or Ambiguous.

→
STEP 4

Serve & learn

Result is stored, analytics-ready. Analyst confirmations become reusable mappings.

Guardrail by design: the engine never forces a match. Uncertain cases go to review — protecting you from wrong merchant attributions.
